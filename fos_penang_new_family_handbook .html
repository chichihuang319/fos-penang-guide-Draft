Penang New Family Survival Guide

Complete 19-Module Orientation Handbook

Welcome to Penang!

Relocating to a new country is an exciting adventure. The Friends of School (FOS) committee has created this 19-module handbook to help your family quickly settle in, navigate local services, and enjoy island life!

1. Welcome & FOS Parent Community

Parent Buddy Program

1-on-1 pairing with experienced families for localized tips and transition advice.

Coffee Mornings

Monthly social meetups to ask questions, share services, and connect with fellow parents.

2. Penang Overview & Orientation

Penang is a vibrant island state offering a mix of modern infrastructure, historic heritage, and tropical landscapes. English is widely spoken in schools, shops, and hospitals.

3. Neighborhoods & Residential Areas

Neighborhood Key Highlights Best Suited For
Tanjung Bungah / Batu Ferringhi Beachfront living, quiet, close to international schools Families prioritizing short school commutes
Gurney / Tanjung Tokong Urban high-rises, shopping centers, premier medical hubs Convenience and modern city living

4. Housing & Rental Guide

  • Lease Terms: Standard 1 to 2 year agreements with a diplomatic clause.
  • Deposits: Typically 2.5 months (2 months security + 0.5 month utility deposit).

5. Visas & Immigration

  • Student & Guardian Passes: Processed via the school admissions office.
  • Passport Validity: Ensure at least 18 months remaining before visa processing.

6. Healthcare & Emergency

  • Penang Adventist Hospital: 24/7 ER with renowned pediatric specialists.
  • Island & Gleneagles Hospitals: Full multi-specialty care and diagnostic services.

7. Transportation & Mobility

  • Grab App: Primary e-hailing app for rides and food delivery.
  • Driving: Left-hand traffic system; International Driving Permits (IDP) valid for 90 days.

8. Banking & Financial Setup

Open local accounts at Maybank or CIMB. Touch 'n Go eWallet is essential for street parking and hawker stalls.

9. Supermarkets & Groceries

Village Grocer

Imported Western goods, organic produce, and bakery items.

Wet Markets

Fresh tropical fruits, local herbs, and fresh seafood at Tanjung Bungah market.

10. Dining & Hawker Culture

Try famous local dishes like Char Kway Teow and Laksa. Always consume filtered or bottled water.

11. School Life & Essentials

Uniforms available at the campus shop. Prepare water bottles, sunhats, and light jackets for indoor AC.

12. Extracurriculars & Sports

Swimming lessons, youth soccer leagues, tennis clubs, and music academies across the island.

13. Family Recreation & Parks

Visit ESCAPE Adventure Park, Entopia Butterfly Farm, Penang Hill funicular, and the Youth Park.

14. Shopping Malls & Retail

Gurney Plaza, Gurney Paragon, and Queensbay Mall offer international brands, cinemas, and dining.

15. Domestic Help & Childcare

Part-time cleaners charge RM20–25/hr. Full-time domestic helpers can be hired via registered agencies.

16. Utilities & Fiber Internet

Subscribe to TIME Fiber or Unifi. Pay electricity bills via MyTNB App and water via PBA App.

17. Pets & Veterinary Care

Import permits required from DVS Malaysia. Veterinary clinics are available in Tanjung Tokong.

18. Culture, Festivals & Holidays

Celebrate Chinese New Year, Hari Raya, Deepavali, and Thaipusam. Schools operate on a 3-term system.

19. Emergency & FOS Contacts

Emergency Services: Dial 999
